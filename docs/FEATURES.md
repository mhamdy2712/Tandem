## Phone as a webcam
Switch **Camera** on from the phone's home screen or from the Camera page on the PC. The PC app shows the live picture and installs a **virtual camera**, so Zoom, Discord, OBS, Teams, the browser and any other program can pick **Tandem Camera** like a normal webcam.
- Choose the quality and frame rate; 60 fps is available when the phone and network can keep up.
- Turn the torch on or off from the PC.
- The picture rotates with the phone. When the PC app is closed, programs that use the camera show a "no signal" picture instead of freezing.

## PC screen on the phone
Open **PC Screen** on the phone to see your PC and use it by touch.
- **Tap** to click, **double-tap** to double-click, **press and hold** then lift for a right click, **press and hold then drag** to drag, **swipe** to scroll.
- **Pinch** to zoom (up to 6×), **drag with two fingers** to move the picture, or use the **+ / −** buttons. The ⌖ button resets the view.
- The **keyboard** button opens the phone keyboard. The view moves to the place you last clicked so the text box stays visible, and a bar above the keyboard shows what you are typing.
- With several monitors, the ▣ button switches between them. Rotated monitors work.
- On Wi-Fi the picture is up to 1080p. Away from home it automatically switches to a lighter 720p stream so it keeps up.

## Phone screen on the PC
Ask the phone to share its screen from **Phone Screen** on the PC; confirm on the phone. You can watch it, take a screenshot and change the volume. In this version it is **view only**. The phone can stop the sharing with the **Stop sharing** button on its home screen.

## Remote Start
Switch your PC on from the phone, from anywhere, and have it sign in for you. Works from shut down, sleep and hibernate when the hardware allows it. See **[WAKE.md](WAKE.md)** for the setup.

## PC control
- Lock, sleep, restart, shut down.
- Volume, mute, play/pause, next, previous, screen off, presentation remote.
- **PC Sound**: play the PC's sound on the phone, choosing which output device it comes from.

## Launcher
List the programs, folders and links you want on the phone, in the PC app. They appear with their icons, and you can **pin** the ones you use most on the phone's home screen. One tap opens them on the PC.

## Files
- Browse the PC's folders from the phone and the phone's folders from the PC, side by side.
- Send files **and whole folders** in both directions, with progress. Drag and drop works on the PC.
- By default a phone only sees your own folders (Documents, Downloads, Desktop, Pictures...). **See all drives** is off until you turn it on.
- Share from any Android app with the system **Share** menu to send straight to the PC.

## Chat
A shared conversation between your phone and PC: notes, links, **pictures and files**. It is saved on both and syncs whenever they connect.
- **Pictures** appear in the conversation. Tap one to open it full screen with pinch and double-tap zoom, **Share**, **Save** to Downloads or **Open with**.
- **Other files** show as a card with a type icon, name and size. Tap to open; press and hold for Open, Save to Downloads, Share or Delete.
- On the PC: open, show in folder or save a copy. Drag files onto the Chat page to send them. Files are limited to 200 MB (use **Files** for bigger ones).
- On the PC you can **choose the folder** where files from your phone are saved (*Change…* on the Chat page).

## Clipboard
Text you copy on one device can be pasted on the other. Turn it off per phone under **Security**.

## PC monitoring
Live CPU, GPU, memory, video memory, network speed, ping, disk activity, temperatures, fan speed and power, shown on the phone and in the PC app.

## Find my phone and location
- **Ring my phone** from the PC, even on silent, with a button on the phone to stop it.
- **Phone location** is **off** until you switch it on. Then the phone reports where it is every few minutes to your PC only, and you can open it in Maps. Android will ask you to allow location "all the time" so it works with the app closed.

## Several phones and PCs
Pair as many as you like. One phone is **active** at a time (the one whose camera, screen and files you are using); switch from the sidebar on the PC or the PC menu on the phone. Messages, status and wake work for all of them.

## Pause
A **Pause** button on the phone's home screen (and on its notification) and one in the PC's title bar stop all connections. Nothing connects until you press **Resume**. Use it when someone else is using the phone or the PC.

## App PIN
On the phone, turn on **App lock** to ask for a 4 to 8 digit PIN when the app is opened. Wrong tries add growing delays, and the app is hidden from screenshots and the recent-apps view while it is on.
