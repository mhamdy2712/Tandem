# Code of Conduct

This is a small project and a friendly space. Be kind and constructive in issues, discussions and any other place connected to Tandem.

**Expected**
- Be respectful of other people and their experience level.
- Keep reports specific and polite, even when something is frustrating.
- Assume good faith.

**Not acceptable**
- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Posting other people's private information.
- Spam or advertising.

Comments and issues that break these rules may be edited, locked or removed, and repeat offenders may be blocked. To report a problem, write to **mhamdy2712@gmail.com**.
