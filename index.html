What it does

Several apps' worth of tools, in one private link.

Pair once with a code. Tandem finds your devices at home over Wi-Fi and away from home through an encrypted relay.

Phone as a webcam

A real camera for Zoom, Discord, OBS and Teams, through a virtual camera on your PC.

See and use your PC

Your PC screen on the phone with touch, zoom and keyboard. Multi-monitor, and lighter automatically away from home.

Remote Start

Switch your PC on from anywhere. Handles sleep, hibernate and shut down, and can sign in for you.

Files, both ways

Browse each device from the other. Send files and whole folders, with progress. Drag and drop on the PC.

Chat with pictures

Notes, links, photos and files between the two. Photos open full screen with zoom; you choose where files are saved.

Shared clipboard

Copy on one device, paste on the other.

Launcher

Your PC's programs, folders and links with their icons, one tap away on the phone.

PC monitoring

CPU, GPU, memory, network, ping, disks and temperatures, live on your phone.

Find my phone

Ring it, even on silent, and see where it was last. Location sharing stays off until you switch it on.

Set up in minutes

Three steps. You only do them once.

Install on the PC

Run the installer. It lives in your user folder and starts in the tray.

Install on the phone

Scan the code the PC app shows. The phone gets the app straight from your own PC.

Pair

Tap Pair and scan the pairing code. From then on, they reconnect on their own.

Questions

Good to know

Is Tandem free?

Yes, for personal use.

Do I need an account?

No. You pair a phone and a PC by scanning a code.

Does my data go to your servers?

No. Everything goes between your own devices, encrypted. The relay used away from home cannot read it, and you can switch it off.

Why does Windows or Android show a warning?

Tandem is installed from a file and is not yet signed with a paid certificate. On Windows choose More info → Run anyway; on Android choose More details → Install anyway. Compare the SHA-256 in the release notes if you want to be sure.

Can I wake my PC when it is completely off?

Yes, with Wake-on-LAN and a network cable, ideally with a small wake station at home. The Remote Start page checks the settings for you.
