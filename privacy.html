Tandem

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026 · Applies to the Tandem apps for Windows and Android and the wake station app.

In short: Tandem works between your own phone and your own PC. There is no account, and the author does not receive, store or sell your data.

1. Who we are

Tandem is developed by mhamdy2712. Contact: mhamdy2712@gmail.com.

2. What stays between your devices

When you use Tandem, the following can move between the phone and the PC you paired, in both directions: camera video, the PC screen, files you choose to send or browse, clipboard text, chat messages, pictures and files sent in chat, the list of apps and programs you pin, battery and storage status, and the phone's location if you switch location sharing on. All of it is encrypted end to end with keys created at pairing. The author cannot read it.

3. The relay server

When your phone and PC are not on the same network, they connect through a relay server run by the author. It only forwards encrypted bytes and cannot read their content. To do this it necessarily sees, while a connection is open: the IP addresses of the two devices, the time of the connection and the amount of data. It does not store your content or keep accounts. You can switch the relay off in the PC app under Remote Start → Away from home.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, a name or an email address.
  • We do not show advertising and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell, share or give your data to anyone.
  • We do not upload your files, screen, camera, location or messages to any server of ours.

5. Data stored on your devices

  • PC: pairing keys (protected with Windows DPAPI), settings, the chat history, files received in chat (in the folder you choose), and an audit log of actions.
  • Phone: pairing keys (sealed with the Android Keystore), settings, chat history and files, and an optional app PIN stored only as a salted hash.

Removing a pairing, or uninstalling the app, removes the keys. Chat files are deleted when you delete the message.

6. Permissions on the phone

PermissionWhy
CameraTo send video to your PC as a webcam and to scan the pairing code. Required.
Location (including in the background)Only if you switch on Phone location from your PC: to show where the phone is, on your PC only.
All files accessTo browse the phone's files from the PC and exchange files.
List of installed appsTo show and start the phone's apps from the PC launcher.
Notifications, battery optimisation, foreground serviceTo keep the encrypted connection to your PC running in the background and to show the required "Tandem is running" notice.
Screen captureOnly when you ask the phone to share its screen with your PC, with Android's own confirmation.

Each permission can be skipped and changed at any time in Android settings.

7. Children

Tandem is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Deleting your data

Your data lives on your own devices. Unpair the phone in Tandem, or uninstall the apps, to remove it. There is no copy on the author's side to delete.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
